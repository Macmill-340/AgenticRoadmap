A practical guide to hand tools.

Every toolbox needs a reliable hammer, a set of screwdrivers, pliers, and an adjustable wrench. Choose the right tool for the call of the job: driving nails calls for a claw hammer, while a rubber mallet protects soft surfaces. Power tools save time on bigger projects; a cordless drill is the most versatile tool a beginner can own.

Tool maintenance matters: clean metal parts, oil hinges, and store each tool dry to stop rust. When a tool breaks, check whether the model has a warranty before buying replacement parts. Good tool habits make every project faster and safer.
